What it is

What is a community token?

A community token is a token that people create and trade on pump.fun, on Solana, in the name of a local business: a café, a shop, a bar, the place everyone in the neighborhood knows.

Every trade on pump.fun pays a small creator fee. When a community token is launched through PunchCard, those creator fees are set aside for the business it was made for. The owner receives them after verifying the business is theirs.

It is not a loyalty program and not a way to pay at the counter. For that, businesses use the PunchCard Club Network.

How it will work

Four steps, no application.

  1. Pick a business

    Yours, or a favorite local spot you want to see do well.

  2. Launch on pump.fun

    Create its community token through the PunchCard Launchpad. It trades on pump.fun like any other token.

  3. Fees are set aside

    Creator fees from trading are set aside for that business.

  4. The owner claims them

    The business owner verifies it's theirs and receives the creator fees.

The benefits

Good for the business, good for its people.

For the business

Creator fees from its community token are set aside for it. There is nothing for the business to pay or set up.

For its regulars

A way to rally around a place you love, tell people about it, and bring its community together.

Built on pump.fun

Tokens launch and trade on pump.fun, on Solana, so anyone with a Solana wallet can take part.

For business owners

Someone made a token for your business?

If a community token was launched for your business through the PunchCard Launchpad, its creator fees are being set aside for you. Once the launchpad opens, you will be able to verify that the business is yours and receive them.

You do not need a loyalty program to claim creator fees. If you would also like one for your customers, you can ask to join the PunchCard Club Network.

Good to know

Before you take part.

  • Not live yet. The launchpad is still being built and tested. Nothing can be launched today.
  • Tokens can lose value. Community tokens are tradable and their price can go down as well as up.
  • Separate from loyalty rewards. Community tokens are on Solana. Loyalty tokens in the PunchCard Club Network are on Base. Holding one gives you nothing in the other.
  • A token is not an endorsement. Anyone can launch a token for a business; that does not mean the business made it or approves of it.
  • Full details before launch. We will publish the complete rules, including what happens to fees a business never claims, before the launchpad opens.

Launches open after testing.
