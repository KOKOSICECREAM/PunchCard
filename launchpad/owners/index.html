Good to know
How long do I have?

Claim within 30 days of the token's launch and you get everything collected so far, plus every future fee. After 30 days, the token's holders share the fees until you claim; claim any time after that and every fee from that day on is yours.

My Google listing has no website

Add your business's own website to your Google Business Profile, then come back. Pages on Facebook, Instagram or similar sites can't be used, because other people can post there.

Where do I put the code?

Anywhere on your homepage (the footer is fine), or in a file called punchcard.txt on your site, or as a DNS TXT record. You can remove it once you're verified.

Can I change my payout wallet later?

No. Once your claim goes through, pump.fun pays that wallet permanently. Use one you'll keep, ideally a hardware wallet or a multisig for the business.

Someone else claimed my business

Report it on the token's page during the 24-hour check and we can stop it.

Do I have to run the token or promote it?

No. You don't have to do anything except claim. A token isn't an endorsement: fans launched it, and you're free to ignore it.

Want a rewards program for your customers too?

That's a separate PunchCard product: ask to join the curated PunchCard Network.
