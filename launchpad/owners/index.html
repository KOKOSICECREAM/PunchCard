How claiming works

Five simple steps. About ten minutes.

  1. Find your tokenType your business name in the search box above and tap Find it. You’ll see your token and how much is waiting for you.
  2. Get a free walletA wallet is an app on your phone, like a bank app that only you control. It’s where your fees are sent. Already have Phantom? You’re set. If not, here’s how to get one in about five minutes.
  3. Show it’s your businessWe give you a short code to paste on your business’s website (your website person can do it in a minute). No website access? Email us and we’ll verify you another way, even in person.
  4. Connect your walletOpen the claim page inside Phantom (there’s a button for it) and approve a free message. Nothing is charged, and no money leaves your wallet.
  5. Get paidAfter a quick 24-hour safety check, your share of everything held is sent to your wallet. From then on, new fees arrive automatically. Nothing to manage.

Prefer some help? We’re happy to walk you through it on the phone or at your business. Just email hello@punchcard.club.

New to crypto?

Set up your wallet in five minutes.

We recommend Phantom: it’s free, popular and easy to use, on iPhone and Android. If you already have Phantom (or another Solana wallet), skip ahead to find your token.

  1. Download PhantomGo to phantom.com/download on your phone and tap the App Store or Google Play button. That way you always get the real app. In the store, check the maker is Phantom Technologies.
  2. Create a new walletOpen the app and tap Create a new wallet. Turn on Face ID or a passcode when it asks.
  3. Write down your recovery phrasePhantom shows you a list of words. This is the master key to your wallet. Write the words on paper, in order, and keep the paper somewhere safe. (Read the safety rules on this page.)
  4. You’re readyThat’s it. Come back to this page on your phone and use Open in Phantom when you claim. Your fees will arrive in Phantom.

Keep your wallet safe

  • Never share your recovery phrase. Not with PunchCard, not with “support”, not with anyone. PunchCard will never ask for it.
  • Never type it into a website or a form. The only place it ever goes is the Phantom app, and only if you’re restoring your own wallet.
  • Paper, not photos. Don’t screenshot it, email it or save it in notes or the cloud. Keep two paper copies in two safe places.
  • Lose it and it’s gone. If you lose your phone and the phrase, nobody can recover the wallet, so keep that paper safe.
  • Sharing your address is fine. Your wallet address (a long code of letters and numbers) is like an account number for receiving. That’s all we ever need.
  • Copy your address from the app, never from your transaction history: scammers send tiny amounts from look-alike addresses to trick you.

Running a bigger business? You can use a hardware wallet (like Ledger) or a shared business wallet instead. The fees go to whichever wallet you choose, permanently, so pick one you’ll keep.

Good to know

Claiming means asking for the business’s share of creator fees already generated by a token someone else launched. It doesn’t mean you launched the token, that you adopt it as official, or that PunchCard represents you, and you can claim and still object to the use of your name (we can remove the token from PunchCard’s listings, though we can’t change or delete the token itself). PunchCard doesn’t guarantee that fees exist or that a claim succeeds. See the Terms.

How long do I have?

Claim within 30 days of the token's launch and you get the business's 90% of everything collected so far, plus of every future fee (PunchCard keeps a 10% service fee). After 30 days, the token's holders share the business's part until you claim; claim any time after that and it's yours from that day on.

Can I share with my fans?

If you like. When you claim, you can choose to give 5–25% of your part to your token's holders, a permanent thank-you shown on your token page. It's optional, and 0% is the default.

My Google listing has no website

Add your business's own website to your Google Business Profile, then come back. Pages on Facebook, Instagram or similar sites can't be used, because other people can post there.

Where do I put the code?

Anywhere on your homepage (the footer is fine), or in a file called punchcard.txt on your site, or as a DNS TXT record. You can remove it once you're verified. Any site builder works (Squarespace, Wix, Shopify, WordPress and others).

I can't edit my website or domain

Email hello@punchcard.club from the business's own email address. We can verify you another way (for example in person or by phone); the claim then goes through the same review hold as any other.

Can I change my payout wallet later?

No. Once your claim goes through, pump.fun pays that wallet permanently. Use one you'll keep, ideally a hardware wallet or a multisig for the business.

Someone else claimed my business

Report it on the token's page during the 24-hour check and we can stop it.

Do I have to run the token or promote it?

No. You don't have to do anything except claim. A token isn't an endorsement: fans launched it, and you're free to ignore it.

How should I talk about my token?

Say what's true and keep it simple. Something like: “Fans created a community token for our shop. We didn't launch or sell it, we don't control its price or trading, and we don't guarantee its value. We receive a share of its creator fees.” If you launched with PunchCard Select, say instead that you agreed to it with PunchCard. It's great to thank your fans and share any perk you offer. Please don't tell people to buy it, or say its price will rise or that you'll support it.

Do I pay tax on the fees?

Fees you receive are generally income for your business, so keep a record of what arrives in your payout wallet and ask your accountant how to report it.
