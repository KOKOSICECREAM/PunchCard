How it works

Three things you can do with the PunchCard app at the shop.

1

Get SKOOP

Open the app, connect your wallet and swap USDC or ETH for the shop's token — right inside the app.

2

Pay at the counter

The team shows a checkout QR. Scan it in the app, check the shop and the total, and confirm.

3

Earn rewards

Pay with the token and rewards arrive automatically. Paying by card or cash? Show your wallet QR.

Install PunchCard

Add the app to your home screen — it opens like any other app. No app store needed.

iPhone

  1. Open punchcard.club/app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Open PunchCard from your home screen.

Android

  1. Open punchcard.club/app in Chrome.
  2. Tap ⋮, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Open PunchCard from your home screen.

Already use a wallet app like Coinbase Wallet or MetaMask? You can also open punchcard.club/app inside its built-in browser.
