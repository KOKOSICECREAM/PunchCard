Overview

Price, supply and where the merchant's tokens are held.

Rewards

Customer rewards paid out by point-of-sale operators on a fixed daily emission. Owner controls can limit or pause payouts; none of them can move tokens out.

Pause payouts Owner

Low-balance alert Owner

Signals on-chain once the undistributed balance falls to this level.

Liquidity

The LP locker's two full-range positions and its token reserve. Positions held outside the locker are not shown.

Add liquidity from the reserve Owner

Pairs USDC and/or ETH from the owner wallet with reserve tokens at the live pool price, within 1% slippage. ETH is wrapped first if needed; unused USDC/WETH is returned and unused tokens stay in the reserve. Added liquidity stays in the locker and its fees follow the locker's rules.

Collect fees Anyone

Collects trading fees on both positions. The token side is burned; the USDC/WETH side goes to the network fee recipient.

Lock permanently Owner

Closes the recovery hatch for good. Liquidity can then never be withdrawn early. Type LOCK to confirm.

Evacuate Owner

Withdraws both positions and the entire reserve to the owner wallet and permanently disables the locker. All or nothing. Type EVACUATE to confirm.

Treasury

Time-locked. Releases pay the owner wallet only, after the waiting period, one at a time.

Release Owner · execute: anyone

Requesting starts the clock; once it has run, anyone can execute and the tokens go to the owner wallet. A pending request can be cancelled at any time.

Vesting

Team allocation: a cliff, then linear vesting. Anyone may trigger a release; it always pays the fixed team wallet.

Network

PunchCard-wide settings held by governance. Changes here apply to every merchant.

Registrars Governance

Keys permitted to admit a reviewed merchant suite to the network.

Router fee Governance

A proposal waits 48 hours, then anyone can execute it with the same values. Ceiling 100 bps (1%).

Governance handover Governance

Move governance to a new key or multisig. Propose from the current key, then accept from the new one once the waiting period has passed. The controller and the router transfer separately.

Wind-down

Freezes rewards, treasury and new liquidity for this merchant. After 365 days the remaining allocations are burned and the LP is released, with 10% kept permanently as a floor. Irreversible.

Start wind-down Governance

Type WIND DOWN to confirm.
