Meet the people

Why we scoop.

We started KOKOS in 2017 with one idea: ice cream everyone at the table can enjoy. Every batch is made by hand from organic coconut milk, and most days you’ll find us behind the counter ourselves.

We know our customers by name, and their kids’ favorite flavors too. That’s the part of this job no machine will ever do for us.

— The KOKOS team
