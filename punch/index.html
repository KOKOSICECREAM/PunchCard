PunchCard Membership

Hold at least $50 worth of PUNCH in your wallet and you’re a PunchCard Member. Members can:

  • Launch tokens on the PunchCard Launchpad, from that wallet.
  • Get 10% off at participating PunchCard businesses (once a day at each), starting with KOKOS Ice Cream. Show a PunchCard Perk Pass at the counter; it checks your wallet live.
  • Nothing is spent. Your PUNCH isn't charged, locked or moved. The launchpad only checks your balance when you launch, and your PUNCH stays in your wallet.
  • Valued at today's price. The check uses the current PUNCH price, so the number of PUNCH it takes goes up and down with the price. The launch page and PunchCard GO show where your wallet stands.
  • Not needed for everything else. Buying tokens, getting holder rewards, a business’s own perks and claiming a token as the business owner never need a Membership.
  • Member discounts are perks. Each business chooses whether to take part, and perks are separate, limited and can change.
  • The amount can change. The $50 level may be adjusted over time. This page and the launch page always show the current amount.

Where PUNCH fees go

Every PUNCH trade pays pump.fun's creator fee. For PUNCH, those fees go to the PunchCard wallet, 7ubE…PTmW, a hardware wallet. Anyone can check it on-chain.

  • Building PunchCard. Development, hosting and the security audit for the Community Launchpad.
  • Buybacks and burns, from time to time. PunchCard may use part of these fees to buy PUNCH and burn it. That's at our discretion, with no schedule and no set amount. It's not a promise, and nobody should expect it or buy PUNCH because of it. Every one is listed below with its transaction.
