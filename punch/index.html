Where PUNCH fees go

Every PUNCH trade pays pump.fun's creator fee. For PUNCH, those fees go to the PunchCard wallet, 7ubE…PTmW, a hardware wallet. Anyone can check it on-chain.

  • Building PunchCard. Development, hosting and the security audit for the Community Launchpad.
  • Buybacks and burns, from time to time. PunchCard may use part of the fees to buy PUNCH and burn it. These are at our discretion, never on a schedule, and not a promise about price. Every one is listed below with its transaction.

At launch, the PunchCard wallet made a 0.5 SOL first buy in the same transaction that created the token.
