How it works for The Nash Collection

Fans trade. NASH gets thanked.

90%of creator fees go to NASHEvery trade of $NASH pays a small creator fee. 90% is paid automatically to NASH’s own wallet, for as long as it trades. PunchCard’s service fee is 10%.
$0to set upNo cost and no commitment. NASH verifies in about ten minutes by pasting a short code into its Shopify site, then picks the wallet that receives the fees.
0things to manageFees arrive on their own. Optionally share a part with holders as a thank-you, or offer a perk, whenever you like.
