Join the PunchCard Club network

Tokenized loyalty rewards for your customers.

Your business gets its own reward token on Base. Customers earn it when they buy from you, keep it in their own wallet, and can spend or swap it at other businesses in the network. We set up everything: the token, a POS for your counter, a customer app and a dashboard.

Who it's for
Business owners who want a loyalty program.
How to join
Send a request. The network is curated, so we review every business.
What it costs
A setup fee. No monthly subscription.
Already live
KOKOS Ice Cream with SKOOP, our first registered merchant.

Launch a community token on pump.fun

A token for a business you love, with its creator fees set aside for that business.

Anyone can create a community token for a local business, whether it's their own or a favorite spot. It launches and trades on pump.fun, on Solana. The token's creator fees are set aside for the business, and the owner receives them after verifying the business is theirs.

Who it's for
Regulars, fans and business owners.
How to start
No application. Launch it yourself once the launchpad opens.
Status
In development. Not live yet.
Good to know
Community tokens are tradable and can lose value.
